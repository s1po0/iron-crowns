Iron Crowns original field materials, 0.5.
Meadow, masonry and steel base-color source surfaces were AI-generated for this
project, then resized, edge-blended and used to derive approximate normal maps.
These are not photogrammetry or scans. Other textures, foliage cutouts and audio
were generated by scripts/build-field-media.py. No Bannerlord assets are used.
Full-resolution source surfaces are preserved in art-source outside the APK.
