# The Western Road — combat/presentation rebuild 0.5

## Start with a visible character
New/continued journeys enter the shared village and battlefield in third person. The hero is
framed prominently, with the camera closer than in 0.4. The default escort forms on the flanks,
leaving the corridor between the camera and hero clear instead of crowding the foreground. The **REALM** button (desktop M) opens
the strategic map; **FIELD CAMP** returns to the physical scene. This is the intended hybrid
structure, but different campaign towns still reuse this one field scene; unique town interiors
are not implemented. Campaign encounters switch into the same scene for real-time combat.

## Controls
| Action | Phone | Desktop |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Left stick | WASD |
| Look | Drag right-side open area | Hold right mouse and move |
| Strike | Hold sword button | Space / left mouse |
| Guard | Hold Block | Q |
| Dodge | Dodge button | Shift |
| Campaign map | Realm | M |
| Formations | Bottom row | 1–4 |
| Pause/settings | II / Android Back | Escape |

Begin Skirmish starts the existing raider encounter. Aiming follows the camera yaw when a
player strike begins; attacks no longer snap instantly onto the nearest enemy. Stand within
reach, face the target and watch the swing. Attacks can miss if the enemy moves away.

## Mechanical changes
- Player windup: 0.23 seconds; enemy windup: 0.34 seconds. Swing/recovery: 0.64 seconds;
  cooldowns: 0.85 seconds player, 1.1 seconds AI.
- Range: 2.05 world units, forward dot-product cone >= 0.35. A collision-layer-1 ray rejects
  strikes through buildings/terrain. Only the nearest opposing live actor is considered.
- Shield mitigation applies only when facing the attacker (dot > 0.25). Normal guard takes
  18% chip damage; a freshly raised player guard has a 0.18-second no-damage parry window.
- Guarded player hits cost 16 stamina; parries do not. Exhaustion breaks guard and staggers.
- Unblocked hits cancel the victim's pending strike and impose a short stagger. Parried
  attackers suffer a longer recovery. This is not physics-based weapon collision or ragdolls.
- Striking costs 12 stamina. Stamina does not regenerate during a swing or dodge.
- Dodge costs 28 stamina and moves for 0.23 seconds. Without movement input it steps backward;
  with input it follows that direction. There are no magical invulnerability frames.
- Enemy guards react to visible attack windups during some defensive windows. AI still uses
  local steering, not sophisticated navigation, tactics or directional attack selection.

## Presentation
Original procedural, human-proportioned armor shells, articulated elbows/knees, heater shields,
and a folded mantle replace the earlier oversized/faceted character design. Materials use
normal-mapped generated surfaces. Trees use cutout foliage, grass is instanced, and the path
is blended into continuous terrain. Distance blending reduces texture noise on distant hills.
The camera traces against solid world geometry to avoid placing itself inside walls, but
this is not a full cinematic camera or advanced collision-volume solution.

The generated meadow/masonry/steel source surfaces are AI-created, not photographs or scans.
The remaining textures, cutouts and sound effects are generated by the repository's media
script. No Bannerlord models, textures, geography or audio are used. Procedural animation
and synthesized audio are not motion capture, field recordings or AAA production assets.

## Mobile and High
Both presets use the same core models and materials. The preset is not a promise of equivalent
frame rate on every phone. Begin with Mobile:
- Mobile: 600 grass clusters, 2x MSAA, 42-unit directional-shadow distance.
- High: 1,800 clusters, 4x MSAA, 70-unit shadow distance.
- Sound can be turned off independently.

Settings are saved in `user://settings.cfg`, independently of the compatible v3 campaign save.
Pause controls are tested through Android touches. The emulator is run without physical audio
output; resource loading/settings are checked, not speaker audibility or sound quality.
Physical-phone FPS, thermals, touch ergonomics and extended combat balance remain unverified.

## Testing and release
Engine checks cover visible hero framing, windup before damage, directional shield protection,
parry, actual stationary dodge, camera obstruction, quality changes and audio resource loading.
The previous campaign/wanderer suite remains. Android smoke checks hero-first entry, campaign
switching, origin/hiring/delivery/save behavior, battle touch controls, pause/settings and restart.
Actual rendered/Android screenshots are reviewed before publication. Automated checks are not
a substitute for subjective visual approval or human combat playtesting.
