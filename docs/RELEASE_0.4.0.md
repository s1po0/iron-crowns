# Iron Crowns 0.4 — The Open Road

An original independent-wanderer game mode for the existing stylized 3D Android prototype. This is not Mount & Blade II, a Bannerlord port, or a mod.

## Play
Create Wanderer → choose Merchant, Pathfinder, or Freeblade → Start My Journey.
Open JOURNAL / JOBS on the map. Hire Mara in Hearthglen, accept sealed letters,
travel to Dusk Tower, then open the journal and Hand Over Letters for 45 gold.
No kingdom pledge, fief, or enemy declaration is required.

## New playable systems
- Three origins with one-time economic, travel, or health benefits and a Samir Farroad backstory.
- A neutral oath: new wanderers cannot assault settlements. Bandit combat remains available.
- Local courier contracts, physical destinations, deadlines, board cooldowns, and one-time rewards.
- Four named tavern specialists with hiring costs, upkeep, scouting/provision benefits or caravan expertise.
- One owned caravan with a visible road party, companion leader selection, arrival-based earnings, daily operating cost and scheduled bandit tolls.
- Up to three town grain mills consuming local stock for daily income. Neither business needs a fief.
- Logbook milestones for visiting 32 settlements/four regions, jobs, profitable grain sales, and business accounting.
- Save v3, with v1/v2 acceptance. Existing campaign saves retain their old conquest access as Veteran captains; new origins are neutral.

## Boundaries
Eight starting soldiers, maximum twelve; one shared third-person battle arena.
Companions are support specialists, not additional named combat models. No tournaments,
full skill trees, six Calradian cultures, cavalry combat, formal kingdom mercenary contracts,
full diplomacy, workshop management interiors, sieges, audio or multiplayer.
Caravan earnings are simplified arrival rewards, not simulated commodity orders. Bandit tolls
occur every seventh campaign day while any of the four raider parties remain; caravans are not
permanently destroyed. Reload returns the owned caravan to its home and restarts its route.
Workshop income suspends for insufficient stock or hostility; no confiscation simulation.

## Install
Android 8+ ARM64 (x86_64 emulator also bundled), offline debug-signed evaluation APK.
A different debug signing key may require uninstalling the previous APK, deleting its local save.
Do not uninstall unless you accept losing that save. Physical-phone performance, long-session
balance and thermal behavior remain unverified. Automated engine, rendered-capture and Android
tests gate publication; results are in the release's BUILD and ANDROID-SMOKE files.
